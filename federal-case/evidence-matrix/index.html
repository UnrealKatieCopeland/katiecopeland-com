Designated ADA Coordinator 28 C.F.R. § 35.107(a)
Required since Jan. 26, 1992 		"Unable to locate any responsive information." Tarrant County Open Records Response · Appendix H [2] Infrastructure Gap
No coordinator identified in response to formal open-records request. Accommodation requests had no designated recipient within the County's ADA compliance structure.
Intake Process for Accommodation Requests 28 C.F.R. § 35.107(b)
ADA Title II, 42 U.S.C. § 12132 		"Court participation accommodation requests are not falling under the County's ADA compliance… the matter [is] closed." Travis Yarbrough, Tarrant County · Appendix F [1] Request Redirected
County ADA office disclaimed responsibility for court-participation accommodations and closed the matter without identifying an alternative pathway or responsible official.
Written Accommodation Determination 28 C.F.R. § 35.107(b)
Tennessee v. Lane, 541 U.S. 509 (2004) 		"Okay. Well, we're not going to do that." The Court · RR Vol. 19, p. 24, ll. 1–7 (May 6, 2024) [3] No Written Decision
No written Title II determination was issued. No entity-level process produced a reviewable accommodation decision before proceedings continued.
Grievance Procedure 28 C.F.R. § 35.107(b)
Required for entities with 50+ employees 		"Unable to locate any responsive information" regarding a grievance procedure for denied accommodations. Tarrant County Open Records Response · Appendix H [2] No Procedure Located
No grievance pathway was identified. Denial of accommodation produced no reviewable administrative record and no avenue for pre-proceeding relief.
Self-Evaluation of Access Barriers 28 C.F.R. § 35.105
Required since Jan. 26, 1992 		"Unable to locate any responsive information" regarding a self-evaluation of court-access barriers. Tarrant County Open Records Response · Appendix H [2] No Record Located
No self-evaluation of court-user access barriers was produced in response to a formal public-records request covering the period since 1992.
Notice of ADA Rights and Procedures 28 C.F.R. § 35.106
Required for public entities 		State's trial counsel argued on the record: "There is no actual legal authority stated within her motion… that this courtroom, this court, is actually subject to the ADA." RR Vol. 19, p. 15 (May 6, 2024) [3] Obligation Disputed
The court's own applicability to Title II was disputed on the record — twenty years after Tennessee v. Lane established that Title II applies to court proceedings. The presiding judge stated: "I'm not sure if we are or not."
Transition Plan / Barrier Removal 28 C.F.R. § 35.150
For structural access barriers 		"Unable to locate any responsive information" regarding a transition plan. Tarrant County Open Records Response · Appendix H [2] No Plan Located
No transition plan for court-access barriers was produced. The State Bar of Texas Courtroom Accessibility Guide — listing CART among available accommodations — was attached to the ADA motion and sat in the record unaddressed.

This is not a dispute about whether a judge made a bad decision. The question is whether any entity ever accepted responsibility for deciding an accommodation request through an ADA process — with a coordinator, a written determination, and a grievance route — before proceedings affecting the applicant's legal rights continued. The County's own documents answer that question.
