2:29 p.m.
The Request for Help
"I am writing to the Local Administrative Judge's office because I am unable to get any family court judge to hear my emergency motions... I have been told only the assigned judge can rule, and she is out of town for weeks. I am a disabled litigant requesting ADA accommodations."
Email from Kathryn Copeland to Local Administrative Judge's Office
2:57 p.m.
The County ADA Office Answers
"Your ADA request would need to go directly through the court your case is assigned to."
Email from Tarrant County ADA Office (ADARequests@tarrantcountytx.gov)
Six weeks later
May 29, 2024
4:58 p.m.
The Escalation Request
"Is there an escalation process within Tarrant County for ADA accommodation requests that are not being addressed adequately by the court? How can I file a formal complaint regarding these ADA violations? Are there specific forms or procedures I should follow? Are there any county or state-level resources or contacts?"
Email from Kathryn Copeland to Tarrant County ADA Office
June 3, 2024
11:35 a.m.
The Final Answer
"The County does not have control over a particular Judge's decision when a litigant seeks ADA accommodations that would modify court proceedings. Such a decision relates to a judge's adjudicative responsibilities and is within a judge's sole discretion."
Email from Tarrant County ADA Office
