Copeland v. Tarrant County · No. 26-10389 (5th Cir.)

The Policy Change.

Tarrant County's ADA accommodation page, documented across five captures from March 2023 through July 2026. The carve-out placing courtroom access within "a judge's sole discretion" did not exist until June 18, 2026 — while the Fifth Circuit appeal was pending.

What the record shows

From March 2023 through September 2025, the County's published policy promised: the ADA Officer reviews your request and responds within 10 business days; if you disagree, you may appeal to the County Administrator within ten business days. No exception for courts. No mention of judges. No sole discretion.

On June 18, 2026 — four months after the district court dismissed this case, and while the Fifth Circuit appeal was pending — the page was replaced with a new version. The 10-business-day review, the County Administrator appeal, and the "Request Review" form were deleted. In their place: a sentence declaring that courtroom accommodation decisions "relate to a judge's adjudicative responsibilities and is within a judge's sole discretion."

On July 1, 2026, the page was modified again. The direct ADA Officer phone number (817-884-1111) and the Relay Texas TDD number (1-800-735-2989) — the only contact methods for deaf and hard-of-hearing callers — were removed.

The key transition — verbatim text comparison

The September 26, 2025 version was confirmed live on June 17, 2026 at 5:59 p.m. (Wayback capture 20260617175904). The June 18, 2026 version was the next capture. You have both sides of a one-day window.

Before Sep. 26, 2025 — live through Jun. 17, 2026

The ADA accommodation process is not for general financial assistance. The ADA accommodation process is for individuals with disabilities who need assistance accessing services, programs or activities made available by Tarrant County, Texas. To request an accommodation for services, programs or activities made available by Tarrant County, Texas, please press the "Request Accommodation" button below and complete the "Tarrant County Public Accommodation Request" form. After you have completed the form, press the "submit" button. The ADA Officer will review your request for accommodation and will respond within 10 business days of the receipt of the request.

REQUEST ADA ACCOMMODATION REVIEW
If you disagree with the ADA Officer's response, you may request that the Tarrant County Administrator review your request for accommodation. To request that the County Administrator review your request for accommodation, press the "Request Review" link below and complete the "Request Review" form within ten business days of your receipt of the ADA Officer's response. After you have completed the form, press the "submit" button. The County Administrator will review your request, the ADA Officer's response, and relevant documentation and will respond within ten 10 business days.

If you prefer to contact the ADA Officer directly, please call 817-884-1111 or through the Relay Texas TDD at 1-800-735-2989.

After Jun. 18, 2026 — first version with carve-out
New opening paragraph (inserted):
"This process does not pertain to complaints of discrimination under Title I of the ADA. The County does not have control over a particular Judge's decision when a litigant seeks ADA accommodations that would modify court proceedings. Such a decision relates to a judge's adjudicative responsibilities and is within a judge's sole discretion."
Deleted: ADA Officer 10-business-day review promise.
Deleted: County Administrator appeal right and "Request Review" form.

Replaced with a new "ADA Grievance Procedures" section establishing a 180-day filing window, 15/30/15-business-day investigation and determination deadlines, and appeal to the ADA Committee — but with no ADA Officer review promise and no County Administrator appeal.

ADA Officer direct line (817-884-1111) and Relay Texas TDD (1-800-735-2989) still present in this version — removed on July 1, 2026.

Legal consequences — three points

1. The carve-out cannot defend the 2023–2024 conduct. It did not exist. Under the County's own published policy when Copeland submitted her April 12, 2024 accommodation request, the ADA Officer owed a written response within 10 business days and she had a right of appeal to the County Administrator. Neither was provided.

2. The removal of the remedial process is evidence of deliberate indifference, not a cure. Voluntary cessation doctrine requires that a defendant demonstrate the challenged conduct cannot reasonably be expected to recur. Replacing a 10-business-day review right with a carve-out — while federal litigation is pending — runs in the opposite direction.

3. Removing the TDD relay number raises an independent § 35.161 issue. 28 C.F.R. § 35.161 requires public entities to ensure that telephone emergency services and general communications are accessible to persons with hearing impairments. The removal of the only TDD contact method from the County's ADA accommodation page is not a court proceeding. The carve-out cannot reach it.

The [JH1.1] marker

Both the June 18 and July 1, 2026 versions of the page contain the string [JH1.1] embedded in the grievance section text: "It may be used by any person who wishes to file a grievance alleging discrimination[JH1.1] on the basis of disability…" This is a Microsoft Word tracked-comment marker — the initials of the person who left a comment at that location, and the comment number. The page was drafted in Word with tracked changes by a person with initials JH and published without cleaning the comment markers. In the trial-level case, this is a document request with a name attached to it: all drafts, revision history, and tracked comments for request-ada-accommodation.html from January 2026 forward, and the identity of "JH."
