Doctrinal Map

  • Tennessee v. Lane (2004): Access to courts is the core of Title II.
  • Crawford v. Hinds County (5th Cir. 2021) & Luke v. Texas (5th Cir. 2022): Framing the structural-failure and participation-injury framework.
  • Strife (5th Cir. 2025): Unjustifiable delay operates as a denial.
  • A.J.T. v. Osseo (9th Cir. 2025): No heightened intent showing required for structural failures.
The Hook: No circuit has squarely decided whether § 35.107's administrative obligations extend to court-participation accommodations. This record presents that open question with the county's own admissions.

Record Status: What is Still Needed

The Gap: While the trial record contains a speech-language pathologist's evaluation documenting "moderate cognitive linguistic deficits" and recommending CART, it lacks a formal forensic expert report concluding that CART was medically essential (rather than just helpful) for effective communication in this specific proceeding.

The Plan: The plaintiff is actively commissioning this precise forensic expert report to complete the evidentiary record before any next accommodation request or new trial-level action.
