**Subject:** Co-counsel / amicus sought — Title II "closed loop" access-to-courts case (N.D. Tex. / 5th Cir.)

Colleagues,

I am a Texas attorney and disability-rights scholar currently litigating my own Title II access-to-courts matters pro se. I am seeking co-counsel, amicus support, or strategic input on a threshold ADA issue currently pending in the Fifth Circuit.

The core issue is a structural **"closed loop"** that I believe has broad significance for litigants with disabilities: a court user requests ADA participation accommodations; the county disclaims authority and routes the request to the court; the court declines to evaluate or rule on the request unless the litigant appears *in person*; and the litigant cannot appear without the accommodation. The access question is never processed *before* proceedings—creating a barrier at the threshold that is established in the institutions' own record statements.

**Posture:**
* **N.D. Tex. (Federal Action):** Title II / §1983 action currently in early stages following recent magistrate rulings.
* **Fifth Circuit (Appeal No. 26-10389):** Emergency applications for injunctions pending appeal drew **two dissents from Judge Haynes**, highlighting the gravity of the access-to-courts question.
* **Related Texas Appeal:** The state appellate court routed the ADA claim to federal remedies while leaving the underlying judgment operative, effectively documenting the "loop" in the state court's own language.

Amicus participation is available in the Fifth Circuit (FRAP 29), and I would welcome co-counsel or consulting help on the federal and appellate tracks. I have a curated record, briefing, and demonstrative exhibits ready to share confidentially with anyone interested in evaluating the case.
