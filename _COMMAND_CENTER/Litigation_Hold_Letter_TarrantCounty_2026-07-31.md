**Re: Litigation Hold and Preservation Demand — *Copeland v. Tarrant County*, No. 26-10389 (5th Cir.); ADA Policy Page Revisions During Pending Federal Litigation**

Dear Mr. Lund and Tarrant County ADA Coordinator:

I write to provide formal notice of a litigation hold obligation and to demand the immediate preservation of electronically stored information and documents described below.

**I. Pending Litigation**

The above-referenced matter is currently pending in the United States Court of Appeals for the Fifth Circuit, No. 26-10389. My Notice of Appeal was filed and the docketing fee of $605.00 was paid on July 28, 2026. My opening brief is due August 31, 2026. The County is a party to this litigation and its counsel of record has appeared in the district court proceedings.

**II. The Triggering Events**

I have documented, through dated captures of the Tarrant County website, that the County's publicly posted ADA accommodation page — tarrantcountytx.gov/en/county/website-use/request-ada-accommodation.html — was materially altered on at least two occasions while this federal litigation was pending:

1. **On or about June 18, 2026**, the page was substantially revised. The revision (a) added a new paragraph stating that courtroom accommodation decisions "relate to a judge's adjudicative responsibilities and is within a judge's sole discretion"; (b) deleted the ADA Officer's 10-business-day review commitment; (c) deleted the County Administrator appeal right; and (d) deleted the "Request Review" form. The prior version of this page — which contained none of these changes — was confirmed live as recently as June 17, 2026 at 5:59 p.m. CDT via Wayback Machine capture (timestamp 20260617175904).

2. **On or about July 1, 2026**, the page was modified again. The direct ADA Officer telephone number (817-884-1111) and the Relay Texas TDD number for deaf and hard-of-hearing callers (1-800-735-2989) were removed from the page.

Both versions of the revised page contain the string "[JH1.1]" embedded in the grievance section — a Microsoft Word tracked-comment marker indicating the page was drafted in Word with tracked changes by a person with initials "JH." This marker was published to the public website and remains present in the current version.

**III. Preservation Demand**

You are hereby directed to immediately preserve, and to instruct all relevant County personnel to preserve, all documents and electronically stored information (ESI) in any format, including but not limited to:

1. All versions, drafts, revision histories, tracked changes, and comments associated with the file `request-ada-accommodation.html` (and any predecessor or successor files serving the same function) from January 1, 2026 to the present, including all Microsoft Word source documents, content management system (CMS) records, and version-control logs.

2. All communications — including email, text message, internal messaging platforms, and voicemail — between any County employee, official, contractor, or legal counsel concerning the content, revision, approval, or publication of the ADA accommodation page from January 1, 2026 to the present.

3. All documents identifying the person or persons with initials "JH" who authored or edited the Word document from which the current page was derived, and all tracked changes, comments, and revision history associated with that document.

4. All communications concerning the decision to add the "sole discretion" carve-out language to the page.

5. All communications concerning the decision to remove the ADA Officer direct telephone number and the Relay Texas TDD number from the page.

6. All records of who authorized the June 2026 and July 2026 revisions, including any legal review or approval.

7. All records of any County self-evaluation, transition plan, or ADA coordinator designation under 28 C.F.R. §§ 35.105 and 35.107 from 2010 to the present.

8. All County ADA grievance records, including any grievance filed by or on behalf of Kathryn Copeland, from 2022 to the present.

**IV. Legal Basis**

The duty to preserve arises upon reasonable anticipation of litigation. *Zubulake v. UBS Warburg LLC*, 220 F.R.D. 212, 216 (S.D.N.Y. 2003). That anticipation existed no later than the filing of the original complaint in this matter. The County has been on notice of this litigation since at least the district court proceedings; its counsel appeared and participated. The revisions described above occurred during the pendency of the federal appeal. Failure to preserve relevant ESI may result in sanctions, adverse inference instructions, or other remedies available under Fed. R. Civ. P. 37(e) and applicable Fifth Circuit authority.

Removal of the TDD relay number from the County's ADA accommodation page also raises an independent issue under 28 C.F.R. § 35.161 (telecommunications), which I reserve the right to raise in the pending appeal or in subsequent proceedings.

**V. Request for Confirmation**

Please confirm in writing, within ten (10) business days of receipt of this letter, that a litigation hold has been issued and that the materials described above are being preserved. Please also identify the County employee or contractor responsible for the June 2026 and July 2026 revisions to the ADA accommodation page, and confirm whether the "Request Review" form at `/en/county/website-use/request-ada-accommodation/request-review.html` remains accessible.
