# Medical Record Analysis — Expert Foundation Summary
*Compiled July 27, 2026 — for use in Ferleger response and DRBA outreach*

## The Four-Layer Expert Foundation

### Layer 1: SSA ALJ Finding 11 (Federal Adjudication)
- **Finding:** "The claimant has been under a disability as defined in the Social Security Act since June 8, 2023."
- **Legal significance:** Retroactively covers every unaccommodated proceeding in the record. The August 2023 trial, the November 2023 default judgment, and the May 2024 final trial all fall within the federally adjudicated period of total disability.
- **Strategic note:** The SSA finding establishes *that* the disability exists under federal law. The clinical records establish *what it looks like functionally.* These are two different arguments and should be kept distinct.

### Layer 2: Neuropsychological Evaluation (March 2025, Dr. Christian LoBue, UT Southwestern)
- **Pre-morbid baseline:** High average (~84th percentile) — single-word comprehension at 99th percentile. This establishes the baseline from which deficits are measured.
- **Key deficits documented:**
  - Sustained attention: impaired
  - Selective attention: impaired
  - Processing speed: impaired
  - **Speeded word retrieval: 1st percentile** (response latency) — slower than 99% of population under time pressure
- **Intact:** Executive functions, visuospatial skills, memory
- **Clinical recommendation verbatim:** "She likely would be vulnerable at times to having difficulty recalling information... Difficulty expressing herself and coming up with words to say could occur at times... She would function best by allotting extra time for things, breaking large tasks into several smaller components."
- **Legal significance:** The 1st-percentile word retrieval latency is the key number for the court-access claim. Real-time oral proceedings without CART, processing time, or written presentation of evidence are functionally inaccessible given this documented deficit.
- **Opposing counsel's likely attack:** The conclusions section notes "the underlying etiology for the current cognitive deficits is unclear but more than likely a combination of multiple factors." Response: the SSA finding establishes disability under federal law; the neuropsych evaluation documents functional presentation, not etiology.

The Reaction Time at Very Low (<2nd percentile) and Complex Attention at 4th percentile are objective, quantified measures of the exact deficits that make real-time oral proceedings inaccessible. This is not self-report — it is a timed computerized test.

### Layer 4: QEEG Report (July 7, 2025, Dr. Alfred Johnson, Johnson Medical Associates, Richardson TX)
- **Key findings verbatim:**
  - "QEEG shows dysregulation in absolute power metrics with elevated current sources over CZ in high beta consistent with clinical findings of CNS inflammation."
  - "Relative power metrics show elevated current sources in theta frontally which indicates issues with focus/concentration."
  - "mTBI shows a **99.0% probability of participation in the known head injury population** with the severity in the mild range."
- **Legal significance:** The QEEG provides neuroimaging-level evidence (EEG-based) of brain dysregulation consistent with mTBI/post-COVID neurological injury. The 99% mTBI probability metric is a striking number. This is the most technologically sophisticated layer of the foundation.

## How to Frame This in the Ferleger Response

The question in the draft was: "What kind of evaluator would you want to see behind a communication-access record like this one?"

**Revised framing:** The foundation already exists at four layers. The question for Ferleger is whether this foundation is sufficient for the trial-level vehicle, or whether there is a gap he would want filled.

**The gap that may exist:** None of these evaluations was conducted *under adversarial conditions* — the neuropsych eval, CNS VS, and QEEG were all clinical settings. What is documented in the trial transcripts (the "I just lost — I have to start my brain back" moment, the mid-trial medical collapses) is real-time evidence of functional breakdown under adversarial conditions. A forensic evaluator who could assess real-time comprehension and expressive capacity under adversarial conditions — rather than a static clinical profile — would fill that gap. But the foundation for such an evaluation already exists.
