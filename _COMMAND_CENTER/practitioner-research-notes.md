## VOCABULARY NOTES — "Words Smart People Will Be Turned On By"

From Gemini Pro validation of the open question:
- **"Shadow docket"** — the term that made SCOTUS procedure legible to non-lawyers. Analogy: we need a term that makes the § 35.107 administrative gap legible.
- **"Deliberate indifference"** — circuits treat § 35.107 violations as evidence of this, not standalone violations. The open question has TWO layers: (a) do the obligations extend to court participation, and (b) does their complete absence = per se violation or merely evidence?
- **"The administrative void"** — candidate term for the § 35.107 gap
- **"The closed loop"** — already in use on the portal; resonates
- **"The referral loop"** — already in use; strong
- **"Structural noncompliance"** — precise, not inflammatory
- **"The infrastructure gap"** — what the System Has No Infrastructure graphic shows
- **"The missing piece"** — already on the Law Has Been Building graphic; validated by Gemini
- **"Court-participation accommodations"** — Gemini's precise term for the distinct legal frontier; use this in outreach to lawyers
