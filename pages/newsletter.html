What You'll Receive

Katie's newsletter is a curated collection of:

  • New Publications — Articles, research, and thought leadership on ADA Title II, meaningful court access, and systems reform.
  • Speaking Engagements — Announcements about upcoming talks, webinars, and conference appearances.
  • Resources & Tools — Updates to frameworks, toolkits, and practical resources for advocates, attorneys, and families.
  • Rare Insights — Occasional reflections on the work, the law, and what it takes to build systems that actually work.

You'll hear from Katie approximately once per month — no spam, no selling your email, no corporate noise. Just meaningful updates from someone doing the work.
