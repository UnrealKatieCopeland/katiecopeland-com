Your Story Matters

Help document family court access barriers. Your experience can change how courts work.

I'm Katie Copeland, an attorney and advocate. I'm collecting stories from people around the world who've been through family court—especially those who faced barriers, asked for accommodations, or struggled to be heard.

These stories are becoming a book called The Invisible Barrier, research that will shape legislation and advocacy worldwide, and training for judges and court staff. But first, I need to hear from you.

Important: I'm not here to help you right now. I can't be your lawyer, and I can't fix your case. What I can do is listen to your story and use it to change how courts work for everyone. If you're in crisis, please reach out to the resources below.

You won't be paid. This is a gift to research and advocacy. But your story could change things.
